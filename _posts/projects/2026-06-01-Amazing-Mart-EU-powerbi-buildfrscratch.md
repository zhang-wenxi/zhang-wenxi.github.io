Most Power BI tutorials just tell you what to click. This one teaches you why it works.
In this walkthrough, you'll build a complete Sales Revenue Dashboard from scratch using the Amazing Mart EU dataset while actually understanding the building blocks behind every step.

What you'll learn:
📥 Importing Excel data into Power BI
📊 Creating & formatting charts with purpose
🧩 How reports are structured conceptually, not just visually
By the end, you won't just have a dashboard. You'll know how to think your way through building the next one.
